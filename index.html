MTG Binder

A free binder for your Magic: The Gathering collection. Open the CSV your card scanner exports (ManaBox, Dragon Shield, TCGplayer) — or one from Moxfield, Archidekt, Deckbox or MTGGoldfish, or a plain spreadsheet — to browse it, see what it is worth, check it against full set lists, draw test hands and goldfish a deck.

Drop your collection CSV or a decklist file here, or

You can pick several files; they combine into one collection, and it is kept in this browser, ready next visit. In Chrome and Edge, allowing edits also saves your changes straight to the file.

Keep it in your Google Drive

Your collection lives in this browser, which means it is on this device only — and a browser may clear its storage when space runs low. Connect your Google Drive and MTG Binder keeps your CSVs in a folder of their own there, so they are on every device you use and backed up where you can see them.

It asks for the narrowest permission Google offers: it can see only the files it makes itself. Nothing else in your Drive is readable by this page.

Paste a link or a decklist

Links to your public lists, one per line: a Moxfield collection (each binder becomes its own folder) or binder, an Archidekt collection or deck, or an MTGGoldfish deck. Or paste a deck as text — from Arena, MTGO, or any "4 Lightning Bolt" list.

Your collection is kept in your browser — and in your own Google Drive, if you connect it. It goes nowhere else; the page only asks Scryfall for each card's artwork and prices, and the sites of the links you paste.
