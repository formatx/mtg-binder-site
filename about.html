About MTG Binder

A free binder for a Magic: The Gathering collection kept as a CSV file: browse it, see what it is worth, check it against set lists, draw test hands and goldfish a deck. There is no account and nothing to pay for.

Open a file exported by a card scanner app, Moxfield, Archidekt, Deckbox or a spreadsheet, or paste a decklist. Whatever columns your file already has are kept, including anything this page does not understand, so it stays usable everywhere else you use it.

Where the card data comes from

  • Scryfall — artwork, prices, set and printing information.
  • MTGJSON — the preconstructed decklists used by checklists.

Neither of them is told anything about you; the page just asks for cards.

Privacy

Your collection stays in your browser. There is no account, no server of ours, and no analytics of any kind. Nothing you open is uploaded to us, and the only place it is ever sent is your own Google Drive, if you ask for that.

A collection file you open is kept in your own browser's storage so it is still there next visit, and — if your browser allows it and you choose to — written back to the file you picked on your own computer. Neither copy leaves your machine. Card images are cached in your browser so the page works offline afterwards.

Google Drive is offered and never required: the page works with no account at all. If you connect it, your collection files are kept in a folder called MTG Binder in your own Drive, and written there as you change them. The permission asked for is the narrowest Google offers — the page can see only files it created itself, which is why a file you drag into that folder by hand stays invisible to it. It cannot read anything else in your Drive, and there is no copy of your collection anywhere but your browser and your own Drive. Disconnecting stops the writing; the files stay yours, in your Drive, and removing one from the page does not delete it there.

The card scanner reads cards on your device. What the camera sees is never uploaded and never stored, and the recogniser that reads the small print along the bottom of a card is downloaded once from this site and then runs in your browser — so reading a card tells nobody that you read it. The list of cards you scan is written to a file in that same Drive folder, and only when you ask for it: that is the one thing the scanner sends anywhere, and it goes to your Drive rather than to us.

The link helper extension, if you installed it, exists only to fetch a card list this page cannot fetch itself. It reads Moxfield, Archidekt and MTGGoldfish card-list addresses, only when this page asks for one, and hands the answer straight back. It stores nothing and sends nothing anywhere else.

Since nothing is held by us, there is nothing for us to delete. Clearing site data for this page removes everything it kept; removing the extension removes the extension. If any of this ever changes, this page changes with it — last updated 29 September 2026.
