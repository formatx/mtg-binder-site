Privacy

MTG Binder, and the MTG Binder link helper extension.

The short version

Your collection stays in your browser. There is no account, no server of ours, and no analytics of any kind. Nothing you open is uploaded anywhere.

The website

A collection file you open is kept in your own browser's storage so it is still there next visit, and — if your browser allows it and you choose to — written back to the file you picked on your own computer. Neither copy leaves your machine.

The page asks two other sites for things, and tells them nothing about you beyond the request itself:

  • Scryfall, for each card's artwork, prices and set information.
  • MTGJSON, for the preconstructed decklists used by checklists.

Card images are cached in your browser so the page works offline afterwards.

The link helper extension

The extension exists only to fetch a card list the website cannot fetch itself. It reads Moxfield, Archidekt and MTGGoldfish card-list addresses, only when the MTG Binder page asks for one, and hands the answer straight back to that page. It stores nothing, and sends nothing anywhere else.

Your data, removed

Since nothing is held by us, there is nothing for us to delete. Clearing site data for this page in your browser removes everything it kept; removing the extension removes the extension.
